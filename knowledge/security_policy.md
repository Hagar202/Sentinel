# Sentinel Demo Co. Security Policy (excerpt)

## Reporting suspicious email
Employees forward any suspicious email as an attachment to security@sentinel-demo.com.
Do not click links, open attachments or reply to the sender before the security team answers.

## Payments and wire transfers
No payment, wire transfer or change of bank details is ever approved by email alone.
Every payment request above 10,000 EGP needs a phone call to a known number and approval in the finance system.
Executives never ask for gift cards or urgent confidential transfers by email.

## Passwords and login
IT never asks for a password or OTP by email, chat or phone.
The only login page for company email is https://login.microsoftonline.com.

## Official domains
The company sends email only from sentinel-demo.com. Any similar-looking domain is fake.

## Response to confirmed phishing
1. Reply to the reporting employee with clear advice.
2. Block the sender domain at the mail gateway (needs approval from the security lead).
3. Search all mailboxes for copies and remove them (needs approval).
4. If anyone clicked or entered credentials: reset the password, revoke sessions, check for mailbox rules.
